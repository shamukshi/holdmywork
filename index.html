welcome to holdmyword

Take care of my work while I go do something else.

“Hold my beer.”
“Hold my tea.”

You say it when you want someone to hold something for you while you quickly go do something else.

HoldMyWord is the same idea — for work.

Let it take care of your work while you step away for a coffee, bathroom break, phone call, or anything else.

You go. Your work keeps going.
